# Berkshire-Hathaway

## Overall Trend

- The stock price increased substantially over the period.
- **Year and Peak Price had a strong positive correlation (~0.94)**, showing a clear long-term upward trend.

## Yearly Performance

- **2015 was the only year with a negative yearly return.**
- The other years showed positive overall growth, although the magnitude varied from year to year.

## Daily Returns & Volatility

- The standard deviation of daily returns was around **0.84%**, indicating relatively moderate day-to-day volatility.
- Daily returns remained fairly consistent over the years.
- The correlation between **Year and Daily Return was almost zero (~0.02)**, which is understandable because daily returns fluctuate rather than continuously increase with the stock's long-term price.

## Drawdown

- Most of the time, the stock price stayed relatively close to its previous peak.
- Most drawdowns were between **0% and 5%**, meaning the stock generally remained around **95%–100% of its previous peak**.
- This suggests that large and prolonged declines were relatively uncommon during the period.

## Volume & Price Movement

- Trading volume varied considerably, with some days showing unusually high activity.
- The correlation between **Volume and Daily Return was very weak**, suggesting that higher trading volume did not consistently result in larger price movements.

## Intraday Behavior

- Analysis of **Open, High, Low, and Close** helped identify the stock's typical daily price range and larger intraday movements.
- The stock generally showed relatively stable daily movement compared with its much stronger long-term price growth.

## Overall Conclusion

The stock showed **strong long-term growth with relatively moderate daily volatility**. Despite occasional declines, it generally stayed close to its previous highs, with most drawdowns limited to 5%.

Overall, the analysis shows that **long-term price growth was much more pronounced than changes in daily returns or volatility**.
